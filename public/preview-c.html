I study Geomatics at the University of Waterloo, with a minor in Computer Science. I've been technical coordinator for the university's AWS Club and its Geospatial Club, running workshops for people who had never opened ArcGIS or a cloud console before.

Most of what I do is spatial analysis in ArcGIS Pro and Python. I've mapped where collisions actually cluster in Kitchener, geocoded 75,267 building permits, and worked out where a wind farm can legally go in Bruce County.

In my free time I build things like PlugNear, a web app for finding EV chargers, and an F450 quadcopter I'm putting together from parts.
