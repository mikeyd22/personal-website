About

I'm in the Geomatics program at Waterloo, which is mostly GIS, remote sensing and surveying, with a Computer Science minor on the side. I've been technical coordinator for the university's AWS club and its geospatial club, which meant running workshops for people who had never opened ArcGIS or a cloud console before.

The drone is the current project. I wanted to understand the hardware that collects the imagery I spend all my time analysing, so I'm building one from parts rather than buying it assembled.
