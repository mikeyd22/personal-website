# F450 drone build, saved page content

Saved 2026-09-13 when the drone page was emptied. Reuse when rebuilding it.

## Components

- **Frame:** F450 (pre-soldered) + landing skid
- **Motors:** 2212 Brushless × 4
- **ESCs:** 20A × 4
- **Propellers:** 9450 × 4
- **Battery:** LiPo 4200 mAh
- **Flight Controller:** Pixhawk 2.4.8
- **GPS:** M8N GPS + mount
- **RC System:** FS-i6X (10 CH) + FS-iA6B
